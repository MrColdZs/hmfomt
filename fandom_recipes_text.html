Recipes are used to cook food in the kitchen. Most of them can either be obtained every Tuesday by watching Dueling Chefs on TV, or are received from befriended townsfolk.

Trivia[]

  • Some recipes can be picked up randomly from the beach or the ground.
  • The recipe for french fries is on floor 255 of the spring mine.
  • The recipe for ketchup is in a message bottle by fishing in the sea with a mystrile fishing rod or higher during spring.
  • "Apple Souffle" is misspelled in the cookbook as "Apple Souffl".
